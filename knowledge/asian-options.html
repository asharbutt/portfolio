Introduction

Asian options are options which look at the average of a underlying asset price over a certain period. These can be fixed strike options, where the strike of the option is fixed and thus the payoff is: $$A_{C} = (A_{t} - K)^{+}$$, or floating strike, where the average becomes the strike itself: $$A_{C} = (S_{T} - A_{T})^{+}$$
