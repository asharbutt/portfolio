Option Greeks

The Greeks for derivative contracts represent the sensitivity of the value of the contract with respect to certain input factors. For Equity options, there are a variety of important greeks which help with risk management, position sizing and quotation. We will discuss the derivation and dynamics of the following greeks:

Delta

The Delta is the change in the value of the option with respect to the underlying. For an equity option, the underlying equity has a delta of 1. The delta can then be defined as:

$$\Delta = \frac{\partial V}{\partial S}$$

Under Black-Scholes, the Delta can be calculated as:

$$\frac{\partial V_{C}{\partial S} = \frac{\partial}{\partial S} [S e^{-qT}N(d_{1}) - Ke^{-rT}N(d_{2})]= e^{-qT}N(d_{1})$$

The Delta of a put is the same but negative i.e. $\Delta = -e^{-qT}N(d_{1})$
