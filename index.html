About

I'm a quantitative finance professional specialising in derivatives pricing, valuation, and financial investigations. I hold an MSc in Quantitative Finance and have spent the past five years working across advisory and product roles at Alvarez & Marsal and S&P Global.

At A&M, my work spans derivatives-focused disputes, regulatory investigations, and compliance engagements. I've repriced interest rate swaps and analysed forward curve construction as part of swap-related investigations, valued and repriced FX derivatives, and worked on disputes involving a variety of firms including a Chilean mining company. In the energy sector, I've worked extensively on LNG market investigations — repricing LNG shipping contracts, researching pricing dynamics across major European gas hubs including TTF, and analysing futures over a five-year period.

Beyond pricing and valuation, I've supported compliance and trade surveillance projects, developed pricing models for business development cases, and applied statistical methods across a range of advisory engagements. I've also built internal GUI tools for curve and volatility surface visualisation.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of rigorous quantitative analysis and practical application — whether that's building a pricing model for a dispute, developing a visualisation tool, or writing a report that translates technical analysis into clear conclusions.
