About

I'm a quantitative finance professional with a background spanning derivatives pricing, portfolio valuations, and financial disputes advisory. I hold an MSc in Quantitative Finance and have worked across both product-focused and advisory roles.

My experience includes portfolio valuations at S&P Global, where I worked within a valuations team focused on complex derivative products, and disputes advisory at Alvarez & Marsal, where I applied quantitative methods to investigations and litigation support involving derivative instruments.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of rigorous quantitative analysis and its application to real-world financial problems — whether that's pricing a complex structured product, building a valuation framework for a dispute, or constructing tools that make these processes more transparent and reproducible.
